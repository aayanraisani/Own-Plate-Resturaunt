Privacy policy

The details that matter, in plain language.

What we collect

Your name, phone number, email, delivery addresses and order history, only to process orders and run your account.

How we use it

To prepare and deliver your orders, contact you about them and improve our service. We do not sell your data.

Sharing

We share details only with the people who deliver your order and with service providers who help us operate, under strict limits.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your account at any time from the account page, or ask us to remove your data.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and keep passwords stored in hashed form.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact page.
